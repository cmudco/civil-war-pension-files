ON THIS . . . 9th . . . . . . . . .day of . . . April . . . . , A. D. 1892 , personally appeared before me
a Notary Public . . . . in and for the aforesaid County duly authorized to administer oaths
Richard Scott . . . . . . . . . . aged . 65 . . . .years, a resident of . Paris Island . .
in the County of . Beaufort . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . and State of . South Carolina . . . . . . . . . .
well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who being duly sworn, declared in relation to
aforesaid case as follows:
That he was well and personally acquainted with
[Note.—Affiants should state how they gain a knowledge of the facts to which they testify.]
the late soldier, Moses Green and served in the same company
and regiment with him; that he is also well acquainted with
his late widow Patsy Heyward and that he was present at
her marriage to the said soldier; that they lived together
as man and wife up to the time of his death on
the 17th day of Feb. 1879
That they were recognized by their neighbors as man and
wife; that Moley Green, their daughter is still living and
is under the care of her guardian, Richard Heyward.
That his means of knowledge of the facts stated is that
from the time of their marriage up to the time of his death,
he lived within three miles of them
His Post Office address is Port Royal S. C. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . He . .further declare that . he has . .no interest in said case and . . . is . . . . . . .not concerned
