                                                            Civil War
                                                                3
                                                              M P B.
                Adams Run, S.C.
                            Sept, 19, 1916

    Department of the Interior,
            Bureau of Pensions,
                Washington, D.C.

    Gentlemen:
            If I could write I would easily
    write you a book in a week's time on
    the movements marches skirmishes and
    fights my Co. experienced and observed.
            I will send you my Poto. picture
    and actually come in person there before
    you if you say so;. I solemnly swear and
    declare I am the man I say I am under
    no. 1,424,473. you give me.
            I can make any one know by what I
    know and can tell, that I am the right
    identical person: Or you can send your
    own government man to me, and I can
    make him know I am the right one.
                            Truly yours,
                                      his
                                 Ben   X   Williams
                                     mark
                                  or
                                 Ben Legree Per W.H.
