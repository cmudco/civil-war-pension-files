Case of  Ben Williams, alias, Ben Legare             No.  1424473.


       On this 30  day of  September  , 19 24, at     Osborn
county of      Charleston        , State of South Carolina   before me,
     E. F. Fewell        , a Special Examiner of the Bureau of Pensions,
personally appeared      John Jerdan             , who, being by me first
duly sworn to answer truly all interrogatories propounded to him
during this special examination of aforesaid claim for pension,
deposes and says:
                                                                      6-5121

 1        My age is 93.  Residence and mail address, Osborne, S. C.  I
 2   have no occupation as I am unable to work.  I am a pensioner, I am
 3   pensioned at the rate of $50 a month onaccount of my service in Co.
 4   A, 35 U. S. C. Infantry in the Civil war.
 5        I know the claimant in this case, Ben Williams, I have only known
 6   him a short time as he does not live in my neighborhood.  I have
 7   talked with this claimant about our experiences in the war and I am
 8   convinced from what he has told me that he served in the 103 U. S. C.
 9   infantry in the Civil war.  I do not remember that I saw the claim-
10   ant while in the service though I may have.  My regiment was in the
11   fight at Honey Hill, S. C.  I could not say whether the 103 was there
12   or not, I do not remember now.  I know that the 55 Massachusetts
13   was there at Honey Hill in the fight.
14        I am convinced from my conversation with the claimant that he
15   was in the service as alleged but I do not remember that I saw him
16   while he was in the service.
17        I am not related to the claimant and I have no interest in his
18   claim.
19        I have heard the foregoing read and it is correct.
