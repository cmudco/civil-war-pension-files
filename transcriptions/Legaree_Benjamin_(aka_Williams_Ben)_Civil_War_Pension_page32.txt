Movements:-  Enlisted at Hilton Head S.C.
took boat and went to Honey Hill S.C. where we
fought for 2 1/2 days. 54th Massachusetts,
Co B. 103 were in that battle also. thence by
land to Bluffton there three or four months and
at Honey Hill about six months and then came
from Bluffton to Fort Polasky. We also went
to Fort Valley ^Ga where we took about 50 prisioners
to Fort Polasky. We went also to Macon,
Ga but no battle there. I rode a big
Iron gray horse named George Gilbert
and was in front giving orders upon that
horse and Sergent Perry was behind
my Co. B. 103 He called the roll and was sec-
ond sergent. I was first sergent. I gathered
the whole Co. for roll Call on foot.
