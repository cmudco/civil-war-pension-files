                                        3-1876

                       DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
                             BUREAU OF PENSIONS
                              WASHINGTON, D. C.
                              Jacksonville, Fla.   November 11, 1924.

The Commissioner of Pensions:

          Caption.  I.O. 1424473 Ben Williams, alias Benjamin Legaree,
Co. B, 103 U.S.C. Inf., address Adams Run, S.C.

          Reference.   Herewith are returned with my report all papers
in the above described case which came to the S.E. Division for
further special examination to show identity, suggesting that
comrades Peter Johnson, Savannah, Ga., Dennis Blake Darien, Ga.
and James Chambers, Ivey Ga. be interviewed to determine whether
they can identify the claimant as soldier.

          Remarks.   Comrade Peter Johnson of Savannah, Ga. died about
five years ago, his wife was seen and interviewed.  She is a
woman about 40 years of age and did not mrrry the soldier Johnson
until 1910, she did not know anything relating to her husband's
service or did not know any of his comrades.  She referred me to
several old soldiers in Savannah who were seen and interviewed,
they referred me to other old soldiers and I kept going from one
to the other, but none of them were able to recall this soldier
Ben Williams alias Benjamin Legaree.  Timothy Judge was again
seen, but he could give no information in addition to his statement
in report two.

          Through information received from the Ivey, Ga. Post office
James Chambers is dead, his widow resides near Ivey.  Dennis
Blake, who several years ago lived in Darien, Ga. is unknown at
the Darien, P.O., it is very probable that he is also dead.  Owing
to the fact that Darien is some fifteen miles from any railroad I
did not deem it prudent to go to Darien to try to locate Dennis
Blake.  If he is still living he is no doubt drawing a pension and
his present whereabouts could be learned through the Disbursing
Office.
