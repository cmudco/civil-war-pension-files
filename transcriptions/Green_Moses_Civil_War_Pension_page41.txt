GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of South Carolina, County of Beaufort, ss:
     In the matter of Minor of Moses Green Co. I.
33rd Regiment U.S. C. I. No. 498, 516
     ON THIS 21st day of January, A. D. 1893, personally appeared before me
a Notary Public in and for the aforesaid County duly authorized to administer oaths
Jeoffrey Green aged 52 years, a resident of St Helena
in the County of Beaufort and State of South Carolina

well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who being duly sworn, declared in relation to
aforesaid case as follows: That he is well and personally
acquainted with Moses Green and his
[Note.—Affiants should state how they gain a knowledge of the facts to which they testify.]
whole family, and know that Molsey
Green is the only surviving minor
child of the soldier and his late wife
Patty Green. That he was a close and
friendly associate of the soldier and his
family and many times slept in their
house at Port Royal and the facts as
stated above are of his own personal
knowledge, and if they were otherwise
he would have known.

His Post Office address is Frogmore, P.O. S.C.,
He further declare that he has no interest in said case and is not concerned
in its prosecution.

I. R. Reed                                     Jeoffrey his X mark Green
Franklin Greaves
