GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of South Carolina, County of Beaufort, ss.
In the matter of the pension claim of Widows No 451,053 of Jacob
Jones 1st Sergeant late of Co. H, 33rd Reg't U. S. C. T. Vols.
PERSONALLY APPEARED on this 15th day of May A. D., 1895, before me,
a Notary Public in and for the aforesaid County, duly authorized to administer oaths.
Susan Jones aged 50 years, whose Post Office address is
c/o J. Mc King Beaufort County of Beaufort
and State of South Carolina, who being duly sworn declares in relation to aforesaid
case as follows: That the claim was filed over
two years ago, and that she has not
heard from the case since August A.D.
1894.
That she is sick, feeble and very depen-
dant for the necessaries of life and an
early pension would greatly assist her;
That she therefore asks that she be
furnished with a statement as to what
evidence is necessary to complete the
claim as she has not been able to
hear from or acquire the status of her
claim from her attorney.
That she has not been aided nor
prompted by any person, written nor
printed statement but that all of
this statement was orally made
to and before R. G. Greaves
at Beaufort, South Carolina
on the date of 15th May A.D.
1895.

I am ~~not~~ interested in said claim ~~nor~~ concerned in its prosecution.
