GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of S. C. , County of Beaufort , ss:
In the matter of Paul Barnwell late of Co H
33rd U. S. C. Inf.

Personally came before me, a Public Notary in and for the aforesaid County
and State, Paul Barnwell claimant , aged 62 years,
citizen of the town of Port Royal
County of Beaufort , State of S. C. ,
well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who, being duly sworn, declares in relation
to aforesaid case as follows: Should think his first attack
of rheumatism was in 1890 or '91 can not state exactly.
NOTE.-Affiants should state how they gain a knowledge of the facts to which they testify.
It was in his knees at first and he had to be lifted from
his bed and back into it he was then at his home
Old Fort plantation near Port Royal S. C. Has been suffering
from it more or less ever since but during
the last five years, perhaps longer, has not been
able to earn his living by manual labor. His
neighbors and friends help him work his land
and assist him in planting, or he could not
manage his crop. He has very little use of his
right arm, sometimes can not use it at all.
He can safely say that his rheumatism was not
caused by vicious habits.
The above affidavit was written in my
presence by E. A. Crofut at Beaufort S. C.
June 12th 1899 from oral statements made by
myself not aided or prompted by any written
or printed statement or affidavit prepared or
dictated by any other person -
................................................ further declares that ................................. ha........... no interest in said claim and ............................ is not
concerned in its prosecution.
