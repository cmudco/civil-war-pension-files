GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of                                     , County of                                     , ss :
In the matter of Wilkins Abbs, Co. I 5 th U.S.C. H. A.
Vols.
ON THIS 16 day of November A. D. 1906, personally appeared
before me, a Justice of the Peace Ex off notary public in and for the aforesaid County and
State, duly authorized to administer oaths Wilkin Abb, aged 63
years, whose Post Office address is Benoit, Bolivar Co Miss and
Onni Lewis, aged 40 years, whose
Post Office address is Bolivar, Bolivar Co Miss
well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who, being duly sworn, declared in relation
to the aforesaid case as follows:
Some years ago, - I think -
in 1879 or 1890 - most certainly some years before
the year 1904 - I became incapacitated for hard work
(I am a farmer) I was examined by Dr Dodge of
Bolivar who pronounced my disease - some disease
of Rectum - He treated me for it - I have
grown worse and am now unable to do hard
manual labor. - I am now living with
my son - and I have ceased to try to farm
myself as I am unable to do the work

NOTE.-Affiants should state how they gained a knowledge of the facts to which they testify.
We the undersigned witnesses - Onni Lewis and
Charly Scott, have known Wilkin Abb for more
than twenty years and of our personal knowledge
know his statement above to be true
we further declare that we have no interest in said case and are not concerned in its
prosecution.
