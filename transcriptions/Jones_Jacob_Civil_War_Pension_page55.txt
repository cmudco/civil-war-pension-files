DEPOSITION

Case of Susan Jones, No. 457.053

On this 28th day of December, 1896, at
Beaufort (Chaplin), County of Beaufort
State of S.C., before me, W. D. Schmelius, a
Special Examiner of the Pension Office, personally appeared James
Chisolom, who, being by me first duly sworn to answer
truly all interrogatories propounded to h____ during this Special Examination of aforesaid
pension claim, deposes and says: ---- my age is 28 years, farmer,
and postoffice address as above.
     I know Susan Jones, clt, and
I also knew her husband Jacob
Jones, for two or three years before
his death.  I did not know either
the clt, or her husband before
1888, but since that time I
have known clt well.  I do
not know anything about
the ages of clt's children.
     Mrs. Jones, the clt, has no pro=
perty except the house in which
she lives and the land about
it.  There is about twenty acres
of this land, but it is very poor
land of little value.  I think
that $100 00/ would be a big price
for the entire place, and all that
is on it.  She has no personal
property, such as horses, cattle or
farming implements, and is
dependent on her own labor
for support.

since the death of her husband
