GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of So. Ca. , County of Beaufort , ss:
In the matter of Isaiah Brown

ON THIS 5th day of August , A. D. 1903, personally appeared before me
a Notary Public in and for the aforesaid County, duly authorized to administer
oaths Dr. M. G. Elliott aged               years, a resident of Beaufort ,
in the County of Beaufort , and State of South Carolina
whose Post-office address is Beaufort, S.C.
well known to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who, being duly sworn, declared in relation to aforesaid
case as follows:

(NOTE.—Affiant should state how he gains a knowledge of the facts to which he testifies.)

I have carefully examined Isaiah Brown & find
that he is lame as a result - of a fracture of femur
of left leg causing the leg to be much shorter than
the right, he is also suffering with - slight muscular
rheumatism & impaired eyesight -
I consider that he is precluded from attending to
daily labors to the extent - of 3/4

also further declares that he no interest in said case and is not concerned in its prosecution.
M. G. Elliott - M.D.
(Signature of Affiant.)

(If Affiant signs by mark, two witnesses who can write sign here.)
