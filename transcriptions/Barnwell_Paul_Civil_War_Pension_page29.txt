             DEPOSITION
Case of Paul Barnwell, Inv. Certf., No. 995901

    On this           1"                  day of   August           , 1902, at
    Beaufort                            , county of  Beaufort
State of  S. C.                     , before me,  Dow McLelain , a
special examiner of the Bureau of Pensions, personally appeared
Paul Barnwell                       , who, being by me first duly sworn to
answer truly all interrogatories propounded to him during this special
examination of aforesaid claim for pension, deposes and says:
1         I am 59 years of age. I enlisted
     in 1863 and was 19 years of age
2    then. I live 4 miles S.W. of Beaufort
     S.C., which is my P.O. address.
3         I was born where I live now
     and have never lived any place else.
     James White and Dick Haywood are
     my near neighbors and have known
     me from my childhood.
4         I was born the slave of J. J.
     Smith and remained his slave and
     and lived with him until free-
     dom. My master is dead. I do
     not know whether any members
     of his immediate family are living
     or not. His sister, Lina Barnwell,
     lives in Beaufort.
5         My father was named Sandy
     Barnwell. My mother was named
     Kate Barnwell. They belonged to
     my owner. Both are dead. I do
     not know where my father got his
     name. I titled after my father.
     I have never been known by any
     other name than Paul Barnwell.
     Lydia Hamilton is my sister. She
     lives near me now. She is older
     than I and belonged to my former
     owner.
