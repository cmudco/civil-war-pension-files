  SHC

                                                  April 8, 1924



          William Fletcher and Company
          Attorneys at Law
          605 F Street, N. W.
          Washington, D. C.


          Dear Sirs:

               In reply to your request of March 26, that prompt action
          be taken on claim for pension, No. 1424473, of Ben Legaree now
          known as Benjamin Williams, based on service in Co. B, 103d
          U. S. C. Infantry, I have to advise you that the claim requires
          special examination to determine its merits.

               As soon as practicable, the papers will be prepared for the
          field and forwarded to an examiner who will undertake to obtain
          the additional information required.  When his report is
          received, further action will be promptly ordered.

                                        Respectfully
                                             Hays Haymaker

                                             Acting Commissioner
