To whom this may concern: I know beyond
any doubt that the Ben. Williams whose applica-
tion for a pension I witnessed before J. W. Mellard
Magistrate of Adams Run, S.C., is the Ben. Legree
or Benjamine Legree (spelling by the sound) who really
did render the service as Sergeant in Co. B, 103
USC. Inf. which he says he did in the application
Further I am not interested in the case by
any relationship, except as true witness and
hope you will pension him at the earliest.
