            Ben Legaree, now known as
            Ben Williams,                                   July 23, 1917.
            Co. B, 103 U.S.C.Inf.



            Mr. Ben Legaree,

                 Care Mr. William H. Patten,

                      Adams Run, South Carolina.



            Sir:-

                 Your above cited claim for pension under the act of

            May 11, 1912, filed May 8, 1917, is dismissed on the ground

            that it is a duplicate of the one filed July 24, 1916,

            and rejected January 17, 1917, on the ground that the evi-

            dence did not show and you were unable to prove that you

            are the person who rendered the service, as Benjamin Lagree,

            in Co. B, 103 U.S.C.Infantry, and no new element has been

            brought into the case.

                                        Very respectfully,





                                                                Commissioner.
