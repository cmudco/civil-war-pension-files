The Commissioner of Pensions:

     Herewith are returned all papers in the above cited claim for
pension together with my report,

     Claim was returned to this district for the testimony of John
Jerdon, Osborne, S. C., as to identity of the claimant with the
soldier of record.

     I called on this witness and took his statement.  He bears a
good reputation and is a pensioner.  He is very old and feeble and
is nearly entirely blind.  Witness has no personal knowledge of
claimant's service in the Army and says he never saw him to his
knowledge while in the service.  He states that he has a long talk
with the claimant when claimant's mind was clearest and he is con-
vinced from what he said that he is the man he claims to be.  Wit-
ness says his regiment was in battle at Honey Hill and that from
claimant's story of the battle he is convinced that claimant was
there.  Other than that he could give no information of value.  I
again made inquiry for other persons who might have knowledge of
claimant's service but could find no one who knew anything about
it.  For years claimant has stated that he served in the Army in
the Civil war and has told most everyone around Adams Run about it.
He is in very bad shape now, old and feeble and is entirely desti-
tute and dependent on charity.

     It appears that there is nothing further to be gained by fur-
ther investigation of this claim and I therefore recommend that it
be referred for the consideration of the Chief of the Board of Re-
view.

                                    Respectfully submitted,

                                           E. H. Fewell
                                           Special Examiner.
