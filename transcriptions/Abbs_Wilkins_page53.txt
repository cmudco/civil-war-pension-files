who, being duly sworn, declared in relation to the aforesaid case as follows:
A short while before I was discharged in Vicksburg Miss. one
night while serving on duty as "Provost Guard" in attempt
ing to arrest some malefactors And while running after them
I fall in a Hole and seriously hurt my right foot I was
mustered out in a few days after and had to go under medical
attention after I was discharged. my foot has been weak and
painful ever since - Also being so young while in service -
carrying my equipments, hurt my back and I suffer
very much from it now,
