In Re:      Cain Brown, Deceased


     Pursuant to an Act of the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina,
approved by the Governor, March 19th, 1915, directing the Judge of Probate to receive
and pay out assets of estates of Five Hundred ($500.00) Dollars, or less, without the re-
quirements of an administration, and without reporting same to the Tax Commission, I,
___Geo. E. Seeley___, Judge of Probate for the County of Beaufort, State of South
Carolina, being informed that the assets of the Estate of ___Cain Brown_________________
in this State, amounts to less than Five Hundred ($500.00) Dollars, have taken charge of
said estate, and hereby demand of _____Treasury of the Uninted States____
that it turn over to me as Judge of Probate any money or other property in its possession
belonging to said estate.

     Section two of the Act is as following:

     "Any person, firm or corporation having in his, her or its possession money or other
property belonging to said estate of such deceased person, shall upon demand of the Pro-
bate Judge turn over same to him, and his receipt for such money or other property to
such person, firm or corporation, shall be a discharge of all liability of such estate."

                                        Geo E Seeley
