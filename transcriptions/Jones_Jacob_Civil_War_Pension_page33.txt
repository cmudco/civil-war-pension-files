Case of Susan Jones                 W.C., No. 437,170.

    On this          30             day of     July       , 1902, at
Beaufort                            county of Beaufort
State of South Carolina, before me, Read Hanna, a
special examiner of the Bureau of Pensions, personally appeared
Susan Jones                             , who, being by me first duly sworn to
answer truly all interrogatories propounded to her during this special
examination of aforesaid claim for pension, deposes and says: I live
in a settlement called Edgerly, about seven miles
from Beaufort. My P.O. address is Beaufort S.C.
I have no occupation. Am pensioned as the
widow of Jacob Jones. Here is my pension cer-
tificate. (Exhibited Act of June 27. 1890. Orig-
inal issue. No. 437,170. Susan Jones, widow
of Jacob Jones. 1 Sergt. "H 33" U.S.C.C.I.
$8. per month to commence Aug. 15. 1890. $2
additional for
William        commencing                             Sixteen
               Aug. 15, 1890.                         Feb. 22. 1892.
Jacob             "     "     "                       July 3. 1894.
        Dated Jan 21. 1897.    R.H. Spec. Ex'mr.)
    (Voucher exhibited for quarter ending Aug 4. 1902.
R.H. Spec. Ex'mr). I don't know my age
but I had given birth to six head of chil-
dren at Freedom. I was born at Coosolatchie.
which is near Yemassee, S.C. Was born on
the plantation of Tom Talbot. I belonged to
Chissy Talbot. My father was Robert Haynes.
My mother was Betsy Haynes. My parents
belonged to my said owner. My owner and
parents are dead. My sister Sophia widow
of Billy Williams, lives where I live.
My maiden name was Susan Haynes. I was
never known by any other name than Susan
Haynes before I married, and I have never
been known by any other name than Susan
Jones since my marriage. I am now
known as Susan Jones. Soldier served
