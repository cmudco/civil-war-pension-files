                  GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of S. C. ,                    County of Beaufort ,
     In the matter of Frederick Brown late Sargeant
     Co D - 33rd Reg't U. S. C. Inf. Vols

     Personally came before me, a Pension Notary           in and for aforesaid County
and State, Frederick Brown                                , aged 55       years
citizen of the town of Port Royal , County of Beaufort      , and State of
South Carolina       , well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and
who, being duly sworn, declare in relation to aforesaid case, as follows:
Has been sick in bed for the last three
     [NOTE.-Affiants should state how they gain a knowledge of the facts to which they testify.]
weeks and not able to attend to getting
my papers ready, as ordered by enclosed
circular letter, Have not been able to
do any work since last September,
Respectfully requests that the delay be
overlooked and the doctor's testimony
herewith attached, be accepted in his
favor.
     The above affidavit was written in my
presence at Beaufort S. C. by E. F. Crofut
from oral statements made by myself
not aided or prompted by any written
or printed statement or recital prepared
by any other person
     ~~further declare~~                             ~~no interest in said case, and~~            ~~not con-~~
