          Office of the Disbursing Clerk,

The Postmaster,
Stovall,
Mississippi.

                 Wilkins Abbs, ( Colored ) with pension number 1144069

            died sometime about last November 1st, 1927; we are holding

Sir:

     Replying to your communication of the 7th instant, re-
porting the death "sometime about last November 1st, 1927"
of WILKINS ABBS, certificate #1144069, I have to advise you
as follows:
     In view of this fact the checks #9262804-AA, dated De-
cember 4, 1927, #9749791-AA, dated January 4, 1928, and
#10234781-AA, dated February 4, 1928, for $65.00, each in
favor of this pensioner, are void for the reason that they
cover periods from November 4, to December 4, 1927, Decem-
ber 4, 1927, to January 3, 1928, and from January 4, to
February 3, 1928, all inclusive, and he died PRIOR to the
dates of issuance of the checks, and they should be returned
to me for cancelation under provisions of the law, an ab-
stract of which was printed on the envelopes containing the
checks, requiring their return in the event the addressee
had died.
     I will thank you to ascertain the exact date of death,
and if he died on or before November 2, 1927, then the check
dated November 4, 1927, #8782814-AA, for $65.00, is also
void, for the reason that he died prior to the last day of
the period covered thereby - October 4, 1927, to November 3,
1927, inclusive, and should be returned to me, in order
that it may be canceled.

     Your courtesy in the matter will be appreciated and I
am enclosing a penalty envelope, which requires no postage
for your reply and the return of the checks.

                                Respectfully,

                                     WALTER JOHNSON.

Encls.                          Acting Disbursing Clerk.
