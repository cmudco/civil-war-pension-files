DEPOSITION

Case of Susan Jones , No. 457,050

On this 26th day of December , 1896, at
Beaufort , County of Beaufort
State of S. C. , before me, W. H. Schmelins , a
Special Examiner of the Pension Office, personally appeared Robert
Frazier , who, being by me first duly sworn to answer
truly all interrogatories propounded to him during this Special Examination of aforesaid
pension claim, deposes and says: my age is 42 years, farmer,
and Postoffice address as above.
I have lived in this immediate
neighborhood all my life, and I have
known Susan Jones ever since I
can remember anything. I remember
as far back as when Sherman's army
passed through this country, and before
that for several years. I cannot say
that I remember Mr. Jones before he
went into the army, but I do remember
him from the date of his return from
the army up to the time that he
died some five or six years ago. I
remember the clt. before I knew her
husband and she was known as
Mrs. Susan Jones from my first
recollection of her, and I know that
her husband was in the army before
I ever saw him. Jacob Jones came
home soon after the close of the war
and he and the claimant lived
together as man and wife from
that time up to the date that Jones
died. I know there was five or six
children born to them after the war
