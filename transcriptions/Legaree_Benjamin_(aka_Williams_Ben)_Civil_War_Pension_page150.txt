                              Memorandum, S. E. Division

     Subject.  1424473 Williams, alias Legaree

     Statement.  The testimony of Comrade Timothy Judge has
been obtained but he apparently does not recall the claimant.
There has been received a communication from one John Jerdon
who states that he knows of the service of the claimant.  The
Record Division index shows that John Jerdon served as a
corporal in Co. A, 35 U. S. C. Inf., and pensioned under
IC C. 839755.

     Reference.  Further examination as recommended in summary,
report 1, for the testimony of John Jerdon, Adams Run, Charles-
ton County, S. C., as to identity.


Aug. 25, 1924

                                          Reviewer.

WBU
