Q     Do you know Geo Chisholm -
a     Yes sir -
Q     Want you living with Geo
      Chisholm when your husband died-
a     Yes sir - I was & that is the
      truth but Geo is married
      now & I don't think that should
      matter -
Q     How long were you living to
      gether before your husband died-
a     Only about one year.
Q     How long did you live together
      after your husband died.
a     About two or three months -
Q     Do you mean that you slept to
      gether & cohabited as man & wife
a     Yes sir - we did -
Q     Why did you live with him -
a     Because I had to leave the soldier-
      he was so mean, no one could live
      with him -
Q     Why did Geo & you separate -
a     Because after the soldier died I
      knew I could not get a pension
      if I lived with a man, so I
      quit him.  I have not lived
      with any one since I quit Geo.
      but I do have men come & have
      intercourse with me, but when
      they get through they go about their
      business - The above has been
      carefully read to me in the presence of
attest the attesting witness & is correct
Emma Hudson                  Rebecca Brown
                                         Deponent.

      Sworn to and subscribed before me this 31 day of Aug,
1898, and I certify that the contents were fully made known to deponent
before signing.
                                   E. H. Jennings
                                            Special Examiner.
