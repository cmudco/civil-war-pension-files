DEPOSITION
Case of Rebecca Brown, No. 632548

On this 23 day of Jany, 1899, at
Port Royal, county of Beaufort
State of S. C., before me, E. A. Jennings, a
special examiner of the Bureau of Pensions, personally appeared
James White, who, being by me first duly sworn to
answer truly all interrogatories propounded to him during this special
examination of aforesaid claim for pension, deposes and says:
I am 75 years of age; my post-office address is Port
Royal S. C.
I knew Frederick Brown
all his life. His first wife
was Mollie. She died about
fifteen years ago. Then he
married Rebecca, the claimant.
No sir, he never was
married but twice.
No sir, I am sure that
he did not marry from
the time Mollie died until
he married Rebecca. I know
this for he was living in
sight of me during that
time When he died -
Rebecca was not living
with him when he died.
The above is correct &
understood -
                                            his
attest -                              James X White
J H Smalls                                 mark
C. C. Sanders

Sworn to & subscribed before me
this 23 day of Jany 1899, & I certify
that the contents carefully made
known to deponent before signing
                                      E. A. Jennings
