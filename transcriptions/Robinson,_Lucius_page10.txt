The Act of June 27, 1890, REQUIRES, in case of a soldier:
   1. An honorable discharge (but the certificate need not be filed unless called for).
   2. A minimum service of ninety days.
   3. A permanent physical disability not due to vicious habits.  (It need not have originated in the service).
   4. The rates under the act are graded from $6 to $12, proportioned to the degree of inability to earn a sup-
port, and are not effected by the rank held.
   5. A pensioner under prior laws may apply under this one, or a pensioner under this one may apply under
other laws, but he cannot draw more than ONE pension for the same period.
