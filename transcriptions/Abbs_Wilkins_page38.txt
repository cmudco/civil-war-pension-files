No. 1.

Declaration for Invalid Pension.

Under the Acts of June 27, 1890, May 9, 1900, and June, 1906.

State of Mississippi , County of Bolivar , ss:
ON THIS 21 day of January , A. D. one thousand nine hundred and eight
personally appeared before me, a Notary Public within and for the County and State
aforesaid Wilkin abbs , a resident of Bolivar
County of Bolivar State of Miss who,
being duly sworn according to law, declares he is the identical Wilkins abbs
who was enrolled as a private Soldier on the 27 day of July 1863 , in and
for Company I 5th , Reg't, US Col artillery (heavy) vol
in the war of the Rebellion, 1861 and 1865, and that he served upwards of ninety days, and was honorably
discharged from said service on the 20 day of May , 1866
That he is now incapacitated from earning his support by manual labor, on account of physical disa-
bilities, and that, to the best of his knowledge and belief, his disabilities, consisting of Rheumatism,
Heart and Lung troubles, are permanent. That his specific disabilities are as follows:
misery in breast. heavy feeling around
heart - suffocation. Dont do heavy work
That he makes this Declaration for the purpose of being placed on the pension rolls of the United
States, under Act of Congress Approved June 27th, 1890, and Amendments thereto. That he has hereto-
fore applied for pension under Application No. dont know That he was not in the
Military or Naval service of the United States prior to July 1863
or subsequent to 20 th May 1866 That he is 62 years of age,
having been born on the ----- day of dont know 1846. That he also claims pension
under the Old Age Order No. 78, as engrafted in Law by Act of Congress Approved June, 1906.
That said disabilities are not due to his vicious habits and are to the best of his knowledge and belief
permanent. He hereby appoints, with full power of substitution and revocation, COS. ALTENBERG,
of Little Rock, Arkansas, his true and lawful attorney to prosecute his claim.
His Post-office address is Bolivar, Miss

M. M. Mason                                          Wilkin his X mark abbs
