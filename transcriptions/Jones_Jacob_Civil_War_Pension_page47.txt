before the war and six after the war. All the
children born before the war are dead. I was
never married before I married Jacob Jones.
I was about 19 or 20 years old then. I was
about a year older than my husband. My
mistress told me so. Jones had never been
married before his marriage with me.
I was never separated from my husband only
while he was in the army. We always lived
together as man and wife from the
time we were married till he died. We
never lived anywhere but on this plantation.
I have not remarried since the death of
my husband, Jacob Jones. I have not lived
or cohabited with any other man as his
wife since the death of Jones.
I had twelve children in all by Jacob Jones.
Six of them were born before the war and
all died young. I had six children after the
war. They are all living. I can't tell their ages.
I can't read or write, and can't remember dates.
I don't know just how old Elizabeth is She
was a good sized girl when her father died.
William was two years younger than Elizabeth.
