                                                  BROWN, Isaiah,
                                                  SC-598 425

Judge George E. Seeley,
Judge of Probate,
County of Beaufort,
Beaufort, South Carolina.


Dear Sir:

          This is in reply to letter of April 14, 1934, from
Washington & Washington, Attorneys at Law, Beaufort, South
Carolina, stating that you have authority to distribute the
estate of Cain Brown, inasmuch as he did not leave an estate in
excess of $500.00.

          Mr. Cain Brown, whose address was Box 63, Beaufort,
South Carolina, filed claim for Government burial allowance
in this case, and upon the evidence submitted, there was found
allowable the sum of $97.00 in his favor in March 1934. The
check covering this amount was returned unclaimed on account
of his being deceased.

          531a  In order that settlement may be made, the enclosed
Form P-121 should be signed by you in duplicate and returned with
a certified copy of the public record of death of Cain Brown.

          All future communications relative to this case
should bear the pensioner's name and refer to the number SC-
598 425.

                                        Respectfully,



                                        E. W. MORGAN,
                                        Director,
                                        Widows' and Dependents'
                                        Claims Service.


encl. Form 531-A in dup.
