GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of South Carolina, County of Beaufort, ss:
In the matter of Susan Jones widow of Jacob Jones
late sergeant Co H 33d Reg't U. S. C. T.
Personally came before me, a Pension Notary in and for aforesaid County
and State, Susan Jones the claimant, aged .................................................................................... years
~~and~~ ....................................................................................................................., ~~aged~~ ..................................................... ~~years~~
citizen of the Town of Edgerly near Beaufort County of Beaufort, State of
South Carolina, well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who,
being duly sworn, declare in relation to aforesaid case, as follows:
I was born at Mitchell H'q near Coosawhatchie
S.C. was taken to Red House plantation S.C.
and lived there with my owner Miss
Crissy Talbird now dead.
Jacob Jones was born at Grey's Hill plantation
S.C. was a yard servant, owned by Mr John
Richardson now dead. Red House and
Grey's Hill plantations are about two miles apart.
Jones and I had been acquainted about
three years when we were married. My
maiden name was Susan Habersham. He
enlisted at Beaufort S.C. in Co. H. 1st S.C. Vols
March 1st 1863 and served till Feb'y 28th 1866.
The 1st S.C. was afterwards 33d U.S.C.T.
We were married before the war by Elder
Dick Nelson, and afterward by Dr Peck.
There are no plantation records now:
all have been destroyed: I have no
marriage certificate. Jacob left me with
six living children: Three are over sixteen
years old. The other three are named
Elizabeth born Dec 6th 1878 William born
April 10th 1880 and Jacob born July 15th 1882.
Jacob Jones was nearly six feet high, dark
complexion, eyes, and hair. Do not know
his exact age: he was about four years younger
than I.
