                                        Charleston S.C.
                                        Sept. 3/98
Sir:-
        I have the honor to re-
turn herewith, the papers in
the claim of Rebecca Brown,
widow of Frederick Brown,
late of Co. I. 33. U.S.C.T.
        This case was re-
ferred for examination as to
legal widowhood.
        Claimants statement
and the evidence show
that she left her husband,
the soldier, at least one year
prior to his death and went
to live with Geo Chisholm
and was living with him
as his wife at time of
soldiers death and for
several months thereafter.
        The claim is with-
out merit and I recom-
mend rejection.
                                Very respectfully,
                                        E.H. Jennings
Hon. Com'r of Pensions,                 Spl. Ex'r
        Washington,
                D.C.
