Hon. Commissioner of Pensions,

         Washington,D.C.

Sir:

             Acknowledging receipt of Bureau letter of the 14"
inst. in connection with the pension claim of Wilkins Abbs Co. I
5" Regt. U.S. C.H.Arty. Ctf. No. 1144069, in which it is stated
that the claim under the act of June 27, 1890 has been rejected
on the ground of insufficiency of evidence to show a pensionable
degree of disability from date of filing, we have the honor to
request reopening of this claim with a view of allowing same.

             It appears that when this applicant was examined Oct.
12, 1892, he then had severe disease of rectum, and the Board of
Surgeons that examined him Nov. 1, 1905 appears to have described
a ratable degree of disability by reason of same, and it is certain-
ly most reasonable to suppose that this disability existed in a
pensionable degree at the date of filing claim in question ~~and~~
~~from~~ Aug. 29, 1904 to date of medical examination Nov.1, 1905.

                              Very truly,

                                   Wm Fletcher & Co
