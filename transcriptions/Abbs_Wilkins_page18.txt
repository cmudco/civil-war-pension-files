                                         Clarksdale, Mississippi
                                         November 15, 1927

Department of the Interior             O. C. 1144,069.
Bureau of Pensions
Washington, D. C.

Gentlemen:

          I am writing you in regard to a pension for the
widow of the late Wilkins Abbs, as private, Co, I, 9th La.
Vols., lst Miss. Arty., and 4th and 5th U. S. Colored H. A.,
and I wish to say that the said soldier died the 4th day of
November, 1927.  He left a dependent widow who has no pro-
perty and who would like to know if under the pension laws,
she would be entitled to a pension from the government.  Her
name is Sallie Abbs and she is living at Stovall, Mississippi,
the place of residence of the late Wilkins Abbs, deceased.

          Thanking you very much to let me hear from you at
once, I am,

                                     Yours very truly,
                                         J H Oneal
