Affidavit.

State of ..................................................... , County of ..................................................... , ss:

   In the Claim of Widow of Moses Green
                                        D - 33 - U.S.C.T.

   ON THIS 30" day of Sept. , A.D. 1892,
before me, a Notary Public , in and for the aforesaid County,
duly authorized to administer oaths, personally appeared Elsy Williams
aged 70 years, a resident of Near Port Royal , in the County of
Beaufort , and State of S.C. , whose Post Office address is
Port Royal
                                            and Jane Barnwell
aged 45 years, a resident of Near Port Royal , in the County of
Beaufort , and State of S.C. , whose Post Office address is
Port Royal , well known to me to be respectable
and entitled to credit, and who being duly sworn, declare in relation to aforesaid case as follows:
We and each of us were well and personally acquainted
with Moses Green, who was a member of Co. "D"
33rd. Regt, U.S.C.T. and also were we, and we are
now well and personally acquainted with John,
Rebecca, and Moses Green children of the said
Moses Green. All three of which children are
now living. We live now, and before the death of
the soldier on the same place with him and
his family. We visited the soldiers wife in confinement
with said children, and that the death of the
soldier occured before any of the said children
reached the age of 16 years. We lived so near to
soldier and family that we used to see them
every day more or less. None of said children has
died since the death of said soldier.
                                            Living



We further declare that we have no interest in said claim, and are not concerned in its prosecution.
S P Deveaux                            Elsy her x mark Williams
Peter Watson                           Jane her x mark Barnwell
