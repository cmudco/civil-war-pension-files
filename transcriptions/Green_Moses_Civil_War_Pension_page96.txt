GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of South Carolina, County of Beaufort, ss:
In the matter of pension claim of Richard Heyward as guardian
of the miners of Moses Green
ON THIS 2nd day of September A. D. 1891; personally appeared before me
a Notary Public in and for the aforesaid County duly authorized to administer oaths.
Mary A Brown aged 65 years, a resident of Beaufort
in the County of Beaufort and State of South Carolina
whose Post Office address is Beaufort S.C.
Georgian Harrington aged 43 years, a resident of Beaufort
in the County of Beaufort and State of South Carolina
whose Post Office address is Beaufort S.C.
well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who, being duly sworn, declared in relation to aforesaid case
as follows:
That we are well and personally ac-
[NOTE.—Affiants should state how they gain a knowledge of the facts to which they testify.]
quainted the soldier Moses Green and
know that he died on the 7th day of
February 1879, and that his wife Patsy
gave birth to a girl child by the name
of Moley on the 18th day of May 1879
& that we both of us were present at
the birth of the child Moley and that
the latter affiant Georgian Harrington
was present and attended the funeral
services of the aforesaid Moses Green
We know that that Moley Green
is the only surviving child of the
soldier. We gain a knowledge of the
above facts from long acquaintance
and being present at the time &
And that the aforesaid Moley is yet still
alive
death
proof posthumous child
living

They further declare that They have no interest in said case and are not concerned
in its prosecution.
