of which I cant recall. We were
in the Honey Hill fight. About
eight of my Co. were killed in the
fight on Folly Island among whom
were Jeffry Robinson. I was never
in hospital nor wounded. I was
just as sound when I came out of
the Army as when I entered it. I never
filed under the general law and
I never intend to for I did not
incur any disability in service
whatever. I was never detached
away from my Co.
Original discharge exhibited. Isaiah
Brown. private Captain Joseph H.
Tibadeaus Co. E 33" Regt U.S.C.V.I.
