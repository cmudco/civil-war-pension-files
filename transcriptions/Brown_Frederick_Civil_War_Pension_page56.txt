GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of South Carolina , County of Beaufort , ss:
IN THE MATTER OF Rebecca Brown, wid. Frederick Brown, late
of Co. D. 33rd. Regt. U.S.C.T.

ON THIS 12th day of October , A. D. 1897, personally appeared before me, a
Notary Public in and for the aforesaid County, duly authorized to administer oaths,
A. M. Simmons aged 35 years, a resident of Port Royal
in the County of Beaufort and State of South Carolina
whose Post Office address is Port Royal S.C.
well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who, being duly sworn, declares in relation to the aforesaid case
as follows: That he is well acquainted with the claimant
Affiants should state how they gain a knowledge of the facts to which they testify.
and with her financial condition. That she does
not own any property in her own right, but has an
interest in a five acre lot, which was owned by her
husband at the time of his death. This land is located
near the town of Port Royal S.C. and is valued at about
$40.00 dollars and personal property valued at about
$75.00, and that she has no income whatever from any
source, save from her own labors.
That his knowledge of the above facts is gained from
personal observations being well acquainted with the family
and living within a quarter of a mile of them for many
years.

His Post Office address is Port Royal S.C.
he further declares that he has no interest in said case and is not concerned
in its prosecution.
