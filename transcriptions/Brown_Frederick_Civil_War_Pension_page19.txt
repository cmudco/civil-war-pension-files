DEPOSITION

Case of Rebecca Brown , No. 632548

On this 1st day of Sept , 1898, at
Port Royal , county of Beaufort
State of S.C. , before me, E H Jennings , a
special examiner of the Bureau of Pensions, personally appeared
T. C. Wallace , who, being by me first duly sworn to
answer truly all interrogatories propounded to him during this special
examination of aforesaid claim for pension, deposes and says:
I am 50 years of age; my post-office address is Port
Royal S.C. Occupation farmer.
I knew soldier all
his life - Knew his first
wife, Mollie - I know that
she died several years before
he married claimant - but
I can not give the year -
I know that soldier
died in Mch. 1896 - I was
present. No sir, claimant
was not present & was
not living with him at
the time - she was living
in Port Royal with Geo
Chisholm at the time. I
know that for she was liv-
ing openly with him and
had been for some time -
She & Geo. continued together
for some time after soldier
died - I dont know how
long - but something less
than a year I think -
Yes sir. I know that Geo
Chisholm & claimant were
living as man & wife at
the time her husband died
& that they lived together for
