I called upon an old friend who fought in the
same Civil war and also in the same battle Especially
at Honey Hill, (S.C.) He was your client you had
served him. He is 94 years of age and nearly blind
now. We both talked together over that war and
that of Honey Hill; Rev. Wm. H. Paden, and a neighbor
of Adams Run, S.C. were with me in the talk to said
John Jerdon at Parker's Ferry, S.C. and I got them
to send you these few words of John Jerdon's in
Statements Enclosed and if you want them
before a notary public, return me and I will get
John Jerdon Co. A. (35) 35th regement to make them
again if he is living. He, as myself is very
feeble, weak, Can't get up to light a lamp
and has a little boy to serve him and for
fear he may die in a few days I got Rev Wm
Paden, to take me in his car to said John
Jerdon last Sunday See his statements. If the same
or another special Committee will come next
week or later, I will get Rev. Wm H. Paden to meet
them at Adams Run S.C. at any train and
return them to it without one penny's expense,
and if needs be return them by state road
to Charleston, S.C. and no expense to it.
He will be at his Presbytery this week returning
next Sunday night. Please hasten any help you can
for me.

Truly yours, Ben Williams, per written request
