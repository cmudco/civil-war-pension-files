DEPOSITION

Case of Susan Jones, No. 457,053

On this 26th day of December, 1896, at
Beaufort, (Ebberly Plantation) County of Beaufort
State of S. C., before me, W. F. Schuckers, a
Special Examiner of the Pension Office, personally appeared Dina Mc=
Knight, who, being by me first duly sworn to answer
truly all interrogatories propounded to her during this Special Examination of aforesaid
pension claim, deposes and says: I am about 80 or 85 years,
no occupation, and post office address
as above.
I have lived in this neighborhood
since I was a young woman. I have
lived here for the past 60 years. I
know the Clt., Susan Jones, and I
knew her husband from the time
they were both little children. I
have known Clt since her birth and
I knew Jones from his childhood.
Clt. and myself belonged to the same
family the Talbirds and were raised
together. I remember when Clt. and
Jacob Jones were married. This
was way before the war. I know
they had five or six children before
the war broke out. They were
married by a colored Deacon, Dick
Kellum. I was present at the marriage.
I saw them married. Clt. was a young
girl when she was married. She
was never married before her mar=
riage with Jacob Jones. Jones
was very young at the time of his
marriage with Clt. He was not any
older than the Clt. They were both young.
Neither had ~~been~~ ever been married before.
