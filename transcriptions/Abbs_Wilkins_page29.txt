The attorney in a recent letter contends that as the examination of
October 12, 1892, showed that the soldier then had a severe disease of
rectum, and as the examination of November 1,1905, appears to have de-
scribed a ratable degree of disability from the same cause, it is rea-

the claim in question August 29, 1904.
    The examination of 1892, shows a pile protruding the size of a
cranberry which was easily replaced, a condition that is certainly not
severe and would not affect the claimant's ability to earn his support
by manual labor in any material degree.
    Furthermore, in a disability which is so varying in nature and
degree as piles, an examination made nearly twelve years before the
claim in question was filed could have little or no bearing on the
question as to the claimant's condition at the date of filing his claim.
