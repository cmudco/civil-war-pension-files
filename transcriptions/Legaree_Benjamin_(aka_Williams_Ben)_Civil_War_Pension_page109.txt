Legaree-Williams                  CGB-                          March 28, 1924.

        Mr. Bell, Re-reviewer
        Mr. McClure, Reviewer

             The fact that no new element has come into the claim since a prior
        rejection, does not in itself necessitate another rejection, if there
        is reason to believe that the former action was error or premature.  In
        this case, the statements made by the claimant, and the corroboration
        afforded by records, rosters, and claims of brothers, justify the belief
        that he rendered the service alleged, and that it would hardly be just
        to deny him the benefit of the special examination which he has asked,
        as he stands ready to make a statement which he believes will convince
        anyone that he is the man who rendered the service.The Reviewer's recom-
        mendation will,therefore, be concurred in.
                                                            I. N. Cuddy
