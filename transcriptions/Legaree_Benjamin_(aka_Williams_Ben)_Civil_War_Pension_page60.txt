            THE AUDITOR FOR THE

                  WAR DEPARTMENT,

                        WASHINGTON, D.C.


      Sir:

                  For use in the above-cited claim for pension,

      please furnish a tracing of soldier's signature from

      the rolls of the organization.    If a claim for arrears

      of pay and bounty has been filed, the loan of so much

      of the evidence as relates to identity is requested.

                  The soldier enlisted January 23,1865 and was

      discharged April 17,1866.
