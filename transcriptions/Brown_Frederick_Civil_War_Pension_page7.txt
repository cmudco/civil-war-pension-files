DEPOSITION a

Case of Rebecca Brown, No. 632548

On this 23 day of Jany, 1899, at
Port Royal, county of Beaufort,
State of S C, before me, E. H. Jennings, a
special examiner of the Bureau of Pensions, personally appeared
I. H. Smith, who, being by me first duly sworn to
answer truly all interrogatories propounded to him during this special
examination of aforesaid claim for pension, deposes and says:
I am 41 years of age; my post-office address is Port
Royal S.C.
I Knew Frederick
Brown from 1865 to his
death - He lived and died
within a quarter of a mile
of my house.
Knew his first wife,
Mollie & remember when
she died, I can not give
the date, but it was five
or six years before he
married Rebecca -
No sir, he was never
married but the two times.
Yes Sir, I am sure that
he did not marry from
the time Mollie died until
he married the claimant
Rebecca -
This is correct and
understood -
No interest nor bias
in this case
I H Smith
