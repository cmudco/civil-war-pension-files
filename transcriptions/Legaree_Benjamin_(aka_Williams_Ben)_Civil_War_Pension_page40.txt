          John Henderson appears to have been 1st Lt.
of Company B to April 1866.

          The following is from the Rebellion Records:

          The 103d was ordered to the District of Savannah,
Georgia, in March, 1865.

          On April 30, 1865, the 33d and the 103d U.S. Colored
Infantry are reported as being in the Savannah District,
under the command of Col. William T. Bennett "Unattached."

          On May 22, 1865, the 103d was still at Savannah, Ga.

          The above is all the information that can be
obtained from the Rebellion Records.

          When the claim is returned by the Special Examiner,
this statement should be attached to the face of his report.
