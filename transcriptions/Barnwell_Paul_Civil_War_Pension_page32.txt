18    Josiah Nowell, of Beaufort, S. C.,
      John Gardner, of Port Royal, S. C., were
      my original witnesses. They were
      not in my Co., but were in my
      Regt. Gardner was in Co. B. Nowell
      was in same Co. They both knew me
      from a boy. I paid them nothing to
      witness for me. I did not witness
      for them. I carried these witnesses
      before the Notary Public and they
      were both sworn when they signed.
      I was sworn each time I signed
      a paper.
19    I have never applied for increase.
      I never applied under the old law.
20    I have not been examined since my
      pension was granted. I have nothing
      pending before the Pension Bureau
      at the present time. I draw for
      rheumatism and heart trouble.
