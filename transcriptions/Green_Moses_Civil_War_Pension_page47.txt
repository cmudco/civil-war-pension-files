GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of South Carolina , County of Beaufort , SS :
In the matter of Pension Claim # 498,516 of Richard Heyward as guardian
of the minor of Moses Green late of Co D. 33rd U.S. C. I.
ON THIS 9th day of April , A. D. 1892 , personally appeared before me
a Notary Public in and for the aforesaid County duly authorized to administer oaths
Simon G. White aged ......... years, a resident of Port Royal
in the County of Beaufort and State of South Carolina
well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who being duly sworn, declared in relation to
aforesaid case as follows:
That he was well and personally acquainted.
[Note.—Affiants should state how they gain a knowledge of the facts to which they testify.]
with the late soldier Moses Green and with his late widow Patty Hey-
ward and knows that neither the said soldier nor his late widow,...
Patty Heyward and knows that neither the said soldier nor his late widow
was ever married previous to their marriage to each other; that he
was present at the time of his death on the 17th day of Feb. 1879;
that they were recognized as man and wife by their neighbors, and
that they lived together as such up to the time of his death; that
Molley Green is the only child that was under sixteen years
of age on the 23rd day of January 1891; that his means of
knowing the facts stated is that he lived on the same
plantation with the said soldier and his late widow from
childhood up, and that he still lives on the same place.
His Post Office address is Port Royal S.C.
He further declare that he has no interest in said case and is not concerned
in its prosecution.
