INABILITY AFFIDAVIT.
To be executed only by the claimant.

State of South Carolina , County of Beaufort , ss:
In the matter of Widows.....Pension Claim No..................
of..Moses Green...., Co. I. 33rd U. S. C. Inf...Vols.
ON THIS....11th.......day of..September.., A. D. 1898 , personally appeared before me
..a Notary Public....in and for the aforesaid County duly authorized to administer
oaths..Patty Haywood........a resident of .Port Royal...
in the County of..Beaufort.......and State of .South Carolina.
whose Post Office address is..Port Royal, South Carolina.....
well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who being duly sworn, declares in relation to
aforesaid case as follows:  That he is unable to comply with the requirements of Pension office as to Medical testi-
mony or evidence showing treatment for or existence of the
rheumatism or disease of heart of her husband
Moses Green from the time of his discharge from
the service to the dates of death for reasons that
the soldier was never treated at said times by any
physician, and relied mostly on the use of a little
patent medicines now and then, and died suddenly.

That she is unable to prove his condition from date of discharge up to the year of death by medical testimony
for the reason that there was no physician in attendance.

That she has not been aided nor prompted
by any written or printed statement, nor by any
person in making this affidavit, and that all
of this statement was orally made to and
written by Isaiah R. Reed Notary Public at
Beaufort, So. Ca, September 11th 1898.

She respectfully requests that the testimony as now on file
...................................be accepted in lieu of..such required testi-
mony......................................................................
