DEPOSITION

Case of Susan Jones, No. 457.053

On this 28th day of December, 1896, at
Beaufort, County of Beaufort
State of S. C., before me, W. F. Schucking, a
Special Examiner of the Pension Office, personally appeared Robert
Goodwin, who, being by me first duly sworn to answer
truly all interrogatories propounded to him during this Special Examination of aforesaid
pension claim, deposes and says: my age is 54 years, farmer,
and postoffice address as above.
I have lived on Edgerly plantation nearly
all my life. I came to this plantation when I
was about two years old and have lived
here ever since. I have known Susan Jones
ever since I can remember anybody, and
I knew her husband, Jacob Jones, in
his life time. I knew him from child=
hood. I served in the same regiment but
not in the same company with him. He
was a member of Co. H and I was in
Co. B - 33d U.S. C.T.
I was not an eye witness to the marriage
of the claimant with Jacob Jones, but
knew of their marriage at the time.
They were married eight or ten years
before the war, and I know there was
four children, and may=be more,
born to them before the war broke out,
Jones and the clt. lived together, and
were known and recognized as man
and wife from the time they were
married till Jones died. They never parted
and always lived here on Edgerly
plantation. I remember the fact of the
births of Clt. children after the war, but
can't give the dates of their births from
