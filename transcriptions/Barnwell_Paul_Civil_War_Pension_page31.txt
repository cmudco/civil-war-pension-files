Paul Barnwell......................................................, who, being by me first duly sworn to
answer truly all interrogatories propounded to h........... during this special
examination of aforesaid claim for pension, deposes and says:
10   Honey Hill, S. C., then back to Charleston.
     From there we went by boat to Sa-
     vannah, Ga., then to Augusta, then
     to Edgefield, S. C., then back to Charles-
     ton, via Columbia. We were over
     at Morris Island awhile and then
     went back to Charleston and were
     mustered out and discharged on
     Citadel Green.
11   I was in the battles of Honey
     Hill, the campaign around Charleston,
     Camp Finnegan, Fla. Sergt. Billy Happy,
     of our Co., was killed at Honey Hill.
     Daniel Field, of our Co. received a
     shell wound at James Island, near
     Charleston.
12   I was not wounded, injured
     or permanently diseased in the army.
     I was never in the hospital.
13   I was with my Co. all the time.
     I never was on detached duty.
14   I was discharged with my Co.
     at Charleston, S.C., because the war
     was over. I gave my discharge
     paper to Mr. Tompkins, of Beaufort,
     S.C. to collect my bounty for me
     and he never returned it. (This
     man is 5- 2 1/2 by actual measurement. black
     No marks or scars.) I was a house
