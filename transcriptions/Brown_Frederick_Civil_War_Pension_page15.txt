DEPOSITION     a

Case of Rebecca Brown, No. 632528

On this 31 day of aug, 1898, at
Beaufort, county of Beaufort
State of S.C., before me, E.N. Jennings, a
special examiner of the Bureau of Pensions, personally appeared
Rebecca Brown, who, being by me first duly sworn to
answer truly all interrogatories propounded to her during this special
examination of aforesaid claim for pension, deposes and says:
I am 37 years of age; my post-office address is Port
Royal S.C. Occupation none
I am the legal widow
of Frederick Brown who served
in Co. D. 33. U.S.C.T -- dont think
he had any other service -
He died near Port Royal S.C.
on the 13th Mch. 1896 - dont know
cause of his death -
I was married to soldier
on the 19th July 1888 - by Rev. A.
Waddell. (Marriage ctf. exhibited
E. N. J.) I was never married
before - He had been married
before - but his prior wife
died six years before he
married me. I have no
children - I have no property
except five acres of land &
two room house - worth
about one hundred dollars.
I have not remarried
since soldier died, and I have
not lived with any man.
Q Were you living with your
husband when he died
A Notsir - I was living in Port
Royal & he was at his house -
Notsir, I was not with him when he died.
