This is to say although I am weak and
sickly in body but fairly strong in mind
and can truly say to your attorney Mr. Wm.
Fletcher and also to the Pension Bureau of the
United States Government that I am
satisfied and certain that the Ben. Williams
alias (Benj. Legaree) is the man and soldier
he claims and says he was and served in
the Civil War in ~~bottle-~~ battle at
Honey Hill, being as he says in Co. B. 103rd
regiment I was there also in battle at
Honey Hill, (S.C.) Co. A. 35th regiment and while I
confess I did not know he was applying
for a pension until lately however I am certain
he served as he says remembering him there in
battle as I do.
            Truly submitted,
