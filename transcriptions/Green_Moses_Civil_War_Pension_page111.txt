          Sir:

               In response to your power of attorney received May 12, 1914,

          you are advised that the above entitled claim for widow's pension

          under the general law was rejected July 11, 1900 on the ground of

          no medical evidence on file showing the soldier's physical condition

          at time of his discharge and thereafter or showing the immedicate
                                                 none
          cause of his death in 1879, and not obtainable, the claimant having

          declared her inability to furnish satisfactory evidence to connect

          the soldier's death with his military service.

               Her claim for pension under the Act of April 19, 1908, filed

          May 10, 1908 was rejected June 4, 1910 on the ground that she had

          remarried prior to date of filing claim.

               The soldier's minor child was pensioned under the Act of June

          27, 1890, certificate number 366, 181, to the time he attained the

          age of sixteen years.

               The soldier never filed a claim for pension.  There is no

          claim now pending on account of his military service.

                                                  Very respectfully,



                                                  Commissioner.

G. M. SALTZGABER.
