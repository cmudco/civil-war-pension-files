Dear Sir:

          Forms 536, 531 and 531-a executed by you, together with a
certified copy of the public record of death, have been received.

          Action on your claim is now withheld pending receipt of
an explanation regarding the discrepancy in the two bills on file, one
in the amount of $110.00 and the other totaling $97.00.  You state that
you paid from your personal funds the amount of $97.00, but the bill of
the undertaker is receipted in favor of the estate of the veteran.  It
is requested that you obtain from the undertaker a statement showing
the correct charge for each service or article furnished and the name
of the person from whom the payment was received.

          The evidence requested herein should be received in this
office within six months from the date of this letter.

          All future communications relative to this case should bear
the pensioner's name and refer to the number S. C. 598 425.

                                        Respectfully,

                                        E. H. Morgan
                                        E. W. MORGAN,
                                        Director, Widows' and Dependents'
                                        Claims Service.
