       Special Examination Division

SHC

                                                      September 8, 1924



       William Fletcher & Company
       Attorneys at Law
       605 F Street, N.W.
       Washington, D. C.


       Dear Sirs:

            Receipt is acknowledged of your inquiry of August 22,
       relative to pension claim No. 1424473, of Ben Williams,
       alias Legaree, Co. B, 103d U. S. C. Infantry.

            In reply I have to advise you that the claim is still
       in process of special examination to determine its merits.
       Reports from South Carolina and Georgia have been con-
       sidered, and the papers are now in the hands of an examiner
       for the purpose of obtaining additional evidence in Charleston
       County, S. C.  When his report is received, such further
       action will be promptly ordered as the evidence obtained may
       warrant.

                                          Respectfully

                                              Washington Gardner

                                                      Commissioner
