          Sirs:

                    You are informed that the above entitled claim
          for pension under the general law, filed June 8,1911, is
          a duplicate of the claim filed April 16,1889,which was
          rejected July 11,1900 on the ground that there was no
          medical evidence to show soldier's physical condition at
          discharge, or thereafter to death, or of the immediate
          cause of his death, and none obtainable, and the claim-
          ant's declared inability to furnish medical or other sat-
          isfactory evidence connecting the soldier's death with
          his military service.

                                        Very respectfully,



                                                  Commissioner.
