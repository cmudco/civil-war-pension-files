                                          December 18, 1925


          Mr. Ben Williams
          Edisto Island,
          South Carolina


          Sir:

               Your claim under the act of May 1, 1920, filed
          November 4, 1925, is dismissed on the ground that it
          is a duplicate of the claim filed March 10, 1924, and
          rejected November 28, 1924, on the ground the evidence
          on file failed to show, and you were unable to prove that
          you were identical with the soldier who served in Co. B,
          103 U.S.C. Inf., under the name of Benjamin Legaree, and
          no new element being brought into the case.

                                          Respectfully,

                                          Winfield Scott
