Civil War Div.                                  Sec.G-GWP-JBG/ GWL
Inv.Orig.1424,473.
Benjamin Lagree, now known as
Ben Williams
Co.B.103 USC.Inf.

                                                September 13,1916.



          Mr Ben Williams,
             C/o Rev. William H. Paden,
                 ADAMS RUN,
                     SOUTH CAROLINA.


          Sir:

                    You are advised that your  claim for pension

          under the Act of May 11,1912, requires proof of your

          identity as the soldier who performed the service al-

          leged.

                    To aid in identifying your self, you should

          give the names of  your company officers, commissioned

          and noncommissioned; and furnish a narrative of your

          recollections of army life, including the movements of

          the company, camps, marches and actions in which engaged;

          and if you had any hospital treatment, state when where

          and for what ailment.  Describe the incidents connected

          with your service just as though you were telling it

          at a campfire.

                    You should forward your original certificate

          of discharge and other papers or letters in  your pos-

          session tending to show your identity as the soldier
