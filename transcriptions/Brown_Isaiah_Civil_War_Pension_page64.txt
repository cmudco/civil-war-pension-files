                                          May 28, 1918.

         Mr. Isaiah Brown,
                       Grays Hill, S. C.-

         Sir:
                       Your claim for increase of pension under
         the general law, filed May 3, 1918, is rejected on
         the ground that you have never been pensioned under
         such law, and, therefore, are not entitled to increase
         thereunder.

                                 Very respectfully,

                                      G. M. SALTZGABER

                                          Commissioner.
