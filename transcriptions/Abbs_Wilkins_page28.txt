        Respectfully returned to the Chief of Southern Division.

        November 20, 1905, an opinion was rendered by this division
to the effect that "the certificate of examination dated April 1, 1905,
shows claimant entitled to a rate under the act of June 27, 1890, for
disease of rectum."  Also that "From a medical standpoint, it is not
shown that such disability existed at date of filing claim."
        March 4, 1908, action was taken by the legal branch of the
Bureau rejecting the claim under act of June 27, 1890, on the grounds
of the insufficiency of the evidence to show a permanent disability
from date of filing.
