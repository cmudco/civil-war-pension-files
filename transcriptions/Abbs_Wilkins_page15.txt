                             COPY.

              MRS. WM. H. STOVALL,
         Merchant  and  Planter,  Stovall, Miss.

                                   Feb. 7th, 1928.


Office of the Disbursing Clerk,
Washington, D. C.

Gentlemen;-

     Wilkins Abbs, ( Colored ) with pension number 1144069

died sometime about last November 1st, 1927; we are holding

his last three pension envelopes and would thank you to

advise as to what disposition we should make of them.

     Old man Wilkins married Sally Gardner just about two

years ago and I understand that she is applying through

some agency, for his pension; personally I advised her to

leave checks here in our safe untill she found out the

correct thing to do.

     I just called Mr. J. H. O'Neal an attorney at Clarks-

dale, Miss; who was handling this matter for Sally and he

advises that Bureau of Pensions would not give pension to

her.

     Checks keep coming; kindly advise disposition and

oblige.                         Yours truly,
                                C. B. Morris- P.M.
                                Stovall,  Mississippi.

                                C. B. Morris.
