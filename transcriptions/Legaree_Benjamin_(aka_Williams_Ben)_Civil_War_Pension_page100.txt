            Adams Run. S. C.
                        Oct. 11, 1916.

The Department of the Interior
        Bureau of Pension
                Washington D. C.

Gentlemen:-
                    Your last card notice
to me was duly received.
     Beg to say in reply, Gentlemen,
I, am feeling old, sick, very weak,
not able to work, can't earn any
living, have no help. Can't pay
house rent, have no money, Can't
borrow any, and hard to get anything
to eat. no clothes. and no shoes.
     I will thank you, Gentlemen, if you
will help me to please help me soon, for
I can't be here long.
            Respectfully Submitted,
                    his
            Benj. x Williams, no. 1,424,473
                   mark
                        Per W.H.P.

P.S. I would have asked your help 20 yrs. ago only I didn't know how.
B. W.
