Neither soldier nor I had been mar-
ried prior to our marriage to each other
We were never divorced nor separated
but lived together till he died. I have
not remarried since he died. I have
lived on Edgerly ever since he died.
Soldier and I had twelve children.
Six were living when he died = Lydia,
Edward, Hagar, Lem, Jacob, Elizabeth.
Jacob is the youngest = I think he was
about four or five at the time of the
"shake" (Aug. 31. 1886. R. R. Shoe. Exmr).
The next youngest is Lem. He is almost two
years and six months older than Jacob.
There was a bible entry of their births
but the bible was destroyed in the 1893
storm. They are still living. I do
not own any property but am wholly
dependent on my pension for support.
Soldier died in May about three years
before the storm of 1893. He died before
I applied for a pension - he died the
May preceding my application. He
died at Edgerly. He died of "dropsy".
"He swelled until he popped." He did
not have an attending physician. He
used to come to Dr Prioleau (dead) and Dr Gibbes (dead)
for medicine. Soldier had been sick
about two years before he died. Soldier
