field hand before enlistment. He was born
on Gray's Hill, which adjoined my said
owners plantation. His owner was John
Richardson (dead). Soldier's father was named old
Galbow Jones (dead). His mother was
Diana Jones (dead). Soldier's brother Adam
Jones lives at Cains Neck. His sister Catherine,
widow of Abram Simmons, lives also at
Cains Neck which is about 12 miles from
Beaufort. Soldier went to Hilton Head
