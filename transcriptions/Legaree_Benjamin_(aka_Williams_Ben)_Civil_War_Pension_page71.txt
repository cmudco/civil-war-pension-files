  Case of      Ben Williams                           No. 1424473.
          _______________________________________

       On this 12 day of    June        , 1924, at  Adams Run
  county of    Charleston               , State of  South Carolina before me,
       E. F. Fewell        , a Special Examiner of the Bureau of Pensions,
  personally appeared   William Paden           , who, being by me first
  duly sworn to answer truly all interrogatories propounded to him
  during this special examination of aforesaid claim for pension,
  deposes and says:
                                                                  6-5121

 1      My age is 52.  Residence and mail address, Adams Run, S. C.
 2  I am a minister of the Gospel.
 3      I know the claimant in this case, Ben Williams or Ben Lagree, I
 4  have known him for about 17 years and I have been trying to help
 5  him some in his pension claim.
 6      Of course I have no personal knowledge of the service of this
 7  claimant but from what I have heard him say I am satisfied in my
 8  own mind that he rendered the service as he has stated.  This man
 9  is very old and feeble and on account of his age he is not able
10  to  gave a very clear statement.  I have often heard the claimant
11  tell of his service and from his talk I believe he actually did
12  serve in the Army in the Civil war.  I know of no one now living
13  who knew the claimant while he was in the service.  He has told
14  me that he had a brother who served in the same regiment.  I never
15  knew this brother but I understand he is dead.
16      I am not related to the claimant and I have no financial interest
17  in his claim.
18      I have heard the foregoing read and it is correct.
19
20                              (Rev.) Wm. H. Paden.
21                                                       Deponent.
22      Subscribed and sworn to before me this 12 day of June 1924,
23  and I certify that the contents were fully made known to deponent
24  before signing.                               E F Fewell
25                                                Special Examiner.
