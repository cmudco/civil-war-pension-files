                                        Charleston S.C. Jany. 25/99.

Sir:-

        I have the honor to return herewith the papers in the

claim of Rebecca Brown, widow of Frederick Brown, late of Co.

D.33rd.U.S.C.T.   No. 632548.

        This case came to me for the purpose of ascertaining

whether the soldier was married after the death of his wife

Mollie and prior to the date of his marriage to claimant.

        The evidence shows that he was not married during that

period, therefore I recommend that the claimant be paid the

accrued pension.

                                        Very respectfully,

                                            E. H. Jennings,

                                                Special Examiner.

Hon.Com.of Pensions,

            Washington D.C.
