                 DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
                         BUREAU OF PENSIONS
                               WASHINGTON, D. C.
                         Jacksonville, Fla.       August 23, 1924.


The Commissioner of Pensions:


     Caption.  I.O. 1424473 Ben Williams, alias Benjamin Legaree,
Co. B, 103 U.S.C. Inf., address Adams Run, S.C.

     Reference.    Herewith are returned with my report all
papers in the above described case which came to the S.E.
Division  to determine whether claimant is identical with the
soldier of record, and came to this district with notice waived
for the testimony of Timothy Judge.

     Manner of testifying.   The only witness seen by me in this
case testified in an apparently truthful manner and without
hesitation, he bears a good reputation for truth and veracity.

     General remarks.   Timothy Judge served in the same company
and regiment in which the claimant served, but he was unable to
call the claimant to mind.  His mind seems to be fairly good,
considering his age, and he stated there were a great many men
with whom he served and who he knew at the time, but not having
seen them since discharge they have slipped his memory.

     Since receiving this case I received a short statement from
John Jerdon, Co. A, 35 U.S.C. Inf., address Adams Run, S.C., it
appears from this statement that Jerdon was in the battle at
Honey Hill and knows of the claimant being in the same battle.

     Recommendation.   To Adams Run, Charleston Co., South Carolina,
for a full and complete statement from John Jerdon, covering the
points at issue.


                                              Respectfully,

                                              M M Wogtman
                                              Special Examiner.
