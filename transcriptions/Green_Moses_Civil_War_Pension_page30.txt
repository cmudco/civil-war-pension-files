                                    ADJUTANT GENERAL'S OFFICE,
                                            Washington, June 13, 1889.

        Respectfully returned to the Commissioner of Pensions.
        Moses Green           , a   Corporal   of Company "F",
33"     Regiment U. S. C. T.        Volunteers, was enrolled on the
1st     day of Nov       , 1862, at Beaufort S. C. 3 yrs,
and is reported: on rolls from enrollment to Apl 17/63
present. Roll ending Apl 30/63 absent "with leave".
May & June 63 absent "sick in Genl Hospital at Beaufort
S. C. since May 1/63. July & Aug 63 present and so
reported to Dec 31/65.
        Mustered out as Private with Co Jany 31/66 at
Charleston S. C.
        Return for July 63 shows him absent sick since
July 16 th.
        He was born in Savannah. Ga. 23 years of age.
5 ft 11 in high, Black Complexion, Gray Eyes, Black Hair,
and a Farmer.
The records of this office afford no additional
information.
                                        J. C. Kelton
                                                Asst    Adjutant General.
