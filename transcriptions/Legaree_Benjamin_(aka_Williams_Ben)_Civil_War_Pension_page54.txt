            Sir:

                      Your claim under the act of May 1, 1920,
            filed Mar. 10, 1924, is rejected on the ground
            that the evidence on file fails to show, and you
            are unable to prove that you are identical with
            the soldier who served in Co. B, 103 U.S.C.Inf.,
            under the name of Benjamin Legree, as alleged.

                                      Respectfully,

                                      Washington Gardner
