                                            BEAUFORT, S. C.

                                            August   13th   1932

The Bureau of Pensions
Washington, D. C.

Gentlemen:
     Several weeks ago, Mrs. Amy Wright forward to your office a claim
for reimbursement, together with a check made payable to Isaac Brown
deceased Civil War Veterans. Inasmuch as she has not yet received any
word from the Department she requested us to write relative to it.

     Kindly acknowledge receipt of the claim and advise as to whether
or not check has yet been issued in favor os the said Amy Wright, daughter
of the said Isaac Brown.

                                       Thanking you an advance,

                                            We are very truly yours,
                                            Washington & Washington
                                            Washington & Washington
