DEPOSITION

Case of Susan Jones, No. 457,053

On this 28th day of December, 1896, at
Beaufort / Edgerly Plantation, County of Beaufort,
State of S. C., before me, W. A. Schuetze, a
Special Examiner of the Pension Office, personally appeared Sophie
Williams, who, being by me first duly sworn to answer
truly all interrogatories propounded to her during this Special Examination of aforesaid
pension claim, deposes and says: my age is about 45 or 46
years. I dont know exactly. Housekeeping
and postoffice address as above. (
    I was born in this neighborhood and
have lived here all my life. I knew
the clt in this case, Susan Jones,
and I knew her husband, Jacob
Jones, from my earliest recollection.
I remember Jacob Jones before he
went into the army though I was
only a child of ten or eleven
years of age at that time. Jones
and the clt were living together as
man and wife when I first knew
them. That was before the war. When
Jones came back from the army
they went to living together again
and continued to live together till
Jones died. Jones has been dead
five or six years. They were never
separated, and were always regarded
and recognized as man and
wife. I cannot give the ages of
clts minor children. I remember
the fact that of the births of nearly
all their children, but the dates
I do not know.
    The clt has not remarried since
