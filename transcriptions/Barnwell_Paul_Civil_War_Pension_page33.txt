23      I execute my quarterly pension
     vouchers before Mr. Crofut, a Notary
     Public of Beaufort, S. C. I go on
     the 4" of each signing month and
     never before. If the 4" falls on
     Sun. I go on Mon. I take both
     papers, sign the voucher three times,
     and am sworn and pay 25 ¢.
24      I have been married but once.
     I married the widow of Milton Edwards
     in 1869. Edwards was dead when
     I married her. I knew him well. He
     belonged to Co. D- of my Regt. I
     knew Edwards and his wife from
     childhood. They both belonged to my
     owner. I knew my wife, who was
     Jane Houston before she married
     Edwards, was never married un-
     but to Edwards and to me. We live
     together now, have never been sep-
     arated or divorced. We were married
     by the Rev. Wilder in his church in
     Beaufort, S. C., before the whole con-
     gregation. James White, Tom Wallace,
     of Beaufort, were both present. We
     have no children under 16 years
     of age.
