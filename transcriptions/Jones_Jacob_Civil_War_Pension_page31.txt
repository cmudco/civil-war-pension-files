We are well acquainted with Susan
Jones the widow of Jacob Jones, and know
that she has not remarried since his
death. She has three children under
sixteen years of age, living with her.
She has a few acres of land which she
tries to plant, but does not get half a living
from it. There is a house on the land
but it was so much damaged by the
August cyclone that she cannot stay
in it. She is dependent on herself for
support.
We know these facts from personal acquaint
ance of many years live on the same
plantation with her and see her often.
This affidavit was written in our presence
by C. A. Crofut of Beaufort S. C. May 8th 1894
from oral statements made by ourselves
not aided or prompted by any written
or printed statement or recital prepared
or dictated by any other person.

We further declare that we have no interest in said case and are
not concerned in its prosecution.
