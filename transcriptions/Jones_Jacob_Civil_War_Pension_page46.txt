DEPOSITION

Case of Susan Jones , No. 457,053

On this 26th day of December , 1896, at
Beaufort, (Edgerly Plantation) , County of Beaufort
State of S.C. , before me, W.R. Schneckins , a
Special Examiner of the Pension Office, personally appeared Susan
Jones , who, being by me first duly sworn to answer
truly all interrogatories propounded to her during this Special Examination of afore-
said pension claim, deposes and says: my age is over 60 years.
I don't know just how old I am. Housekeeping
and postoffice address as above.
I am the widow of Jacob Jones, and
clt in this case.
Jacob Jones was a member of Co. H-33d
U.S.C.T. I do not know the exact date of either
his enlistment or his discharge from the service,
but the war had been going on for over a
year when he enlisted, and he did not
get home again till in the winter after the
close of the war.
My husband never served in the military
or naval service of the United States ex=
cept in Co. H- 33d U.S.C.T.
My husband died here on this plantation
May 15, 1890. He died of "disease of heart,
shortness of breath and Asthma"
Both my husband and myself were
slaves before the war and before we were
married. I belonged to Miss Chrissy Talbird
(now dead), and Jacob Jones belonged to Mrs.
Sarah Barnwell, (now dead). We lived on ad=
joining plantations here, and were married
by consent of our owners. We were married
