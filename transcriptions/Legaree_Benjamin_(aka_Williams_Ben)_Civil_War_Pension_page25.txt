               Adams Run, S. C.
                       July 21, 1916

  To The Department of the Interior
         Bureau of Pension,
                          Washington, D.C.

Gentlemen:-
            I beg leave to say concerning
the application for a pension for Ben William or
Ben. Legaree as registered in his Master's name
in the Civil War I am satisfied he rendered
the service, and is the identical person, and
being now old and very needy, unable to earn
a living, that if it is possible for him to
receive any bounty aid or otherwise so as
to enable him provide him a little home
and cut off renting, he would be greatly
encouraged and benefitted and also the
community. Hoping you may grant him help
  I am, Yours Respectfully
              (Rev.) Wm H. Paden.
           3   Presbyterian Minister
                        of
               the Gospel.
