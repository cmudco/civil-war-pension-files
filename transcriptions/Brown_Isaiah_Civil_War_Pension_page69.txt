                                            April 14th., 1934

The Veterans Administration
Central Office
Washington, D. C.

Gentlemen:

We are herewith enclosing a check in the amount of Ninety Seven
Dollars, together with an Order signed by the Probate Judge of
Beaufort, South Carolina, for the following reasons; to wit:
The Check was made payable to one Cain Brown, as a reimbursement
for certain monies which he paid Chas. Riley, Undertaker for
      Isaiah
Isiah Brown, deceased Civil War Veteran. On the exact day which
your notification came as to the approval of his claim, he was
buried. Soon after that time the check was received by us, that
is in care of Box 63. We believe that inasmuch as the check was
drawn before his death, that his widow and child is entitled
to what he should have gotton, and therefore it becomes a part
of his estate. Under the Act of March 19th,1915 of the South Carolina
Code, the Probate Judge has the authority to distrubute any estate
which amounts to less than Five Hundred Dollars, without the require
ment of an Administraton. Cain Brown, the deceased, did not leave
an Estate in that amount, hence, we took this stept , in his behalf.
Kindly give us your opinon in that matter.

Thanking you.
                                Very Respectfully,

                                Washington & Washington
    J. I. W.                    Washington & Washington.

P.S. We did not know of his death until
some days after. He was injured and lived only a day
after his injury.
