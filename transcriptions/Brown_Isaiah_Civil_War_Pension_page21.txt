Dear Sir:

         This will acknowledge receipt of an affidavit and a state-
ment of expenses incurred in connection with the death of the
veteran named.

         The bill submitted with your claim is rendered by
C.S. Riley, 314 Duke Street, Beaufort, South Carolina, in amount
of $100.00, and is receipted to the estate. Reimbursement may be
made only to the person who used his personal funds in the payment
of the bill.  The bill submitted by Amy Wright, the daughter, in
her claim for reimbursement of accrued pension, is rendered by
H. G. Fisher, 42 West Street, Beaufort, South Carolina, in amount
of $97.00.  The discrepancy in the bills must be explained and
information then furnished to show whether the bill was paid from
the funds of the deceased or from your personal funds as stated
on the affidavit executed April 27, 1933.

         The enclosed forms 536, 531 and 531-A should be
completed as directed by the printed instructions.  The death
certificate which must be certified as a true copy over the
signature of the legal custodian of death records is required. The
death certificate is not found in the Pension Bureau files.

         All future communications relative to this case should
bear the pensioner's name and refer to the number S.C.598,425.

                                Respectfully,



Encl.                           GEORGE E. BROWN,
                                Director of Compensation.
