       S.d. Division.
            H.G.S.

                    DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR,

                         BUREAU OF PENSIONS,

                              WASHINGTON, D. C.,   November 19, 1897.



Mrs. Rebecca Brown,

     Port Royal, South Carolina.

Madam:

     Upon request of Hon. William Elliott, you are advised that

your pension claim No. 632,548, as widow of Frederick Brown, Co. D,

33d U. S. V. l., requires special examination, the evidence on file

not warranting adjudication.

     The case has been referred to a Special Examiner, who will

dispose of the matter at the earliest date practicable, it being

hoped that his inquiries may result in final action.

                              Very respectfully,

                                   J. L. Davenport
                                   Acting Commissioner.
