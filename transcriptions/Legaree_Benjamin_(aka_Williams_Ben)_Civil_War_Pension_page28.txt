          Adams Run S. C. Sept. 19, 1916.
    Department of Interior
        Bureau of Pensions,
                Washington, D. C.
Gentlemen:-
          Your letter to me through Acting Com-
missioner, E. C. Tieman or Tremon is duly
to hand and noted with Care, dated Sept. 13, 1916.
     I have, at Considerable expenses to me
to have no money sent you in two letters
almost everything you are now asking
me herein; for I went before Magistrate D. W.
Mellard here, filled in the Application your
Bureau sent me through Mr. G. M. Saltzgaber
and then and there had two of my old well
known Comrads to both identify and witness
me and my Application true in every way.
They know me. They know I am the very
identical man who provided the sevice in
Co. B. 103 U.S.C. Inf. the same sworn to and sent
you, and I am now going to tell you again
to the best of my ability, Calling and Spelling
names by the sounds for I can't write nor
spell.

dered. I would not Try to do So. - over 3 -

STAMPS
