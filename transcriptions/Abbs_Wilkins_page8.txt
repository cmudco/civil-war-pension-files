Widow Division
S.C. 114,069
Wilkins Abbs
I-5th U.S.C.H.A.

                                            June 19, 1930.





          Mrs. Nelly O'Brien,
          American Red Cross,
          Clarksdale, Miss.


          Dear Madam:

                  In response to your letter relative to a pension
          for Mrs. Sallie Gordon Abbs, I beg to advise you that in
          order to have title to pension under the Act of May 1, 1920
          as widow of a soldier the claimant must have been married
          to him prior to June 27, 1905, and as you state that Mrs.
          Abbs was not married until June 26, 1926 she would therefore
          have no title to pension under the Act of May 1, 1920, and
          it would be of no benefit to her to file a claim for pension.

                                      Very truly yours,





                                              E. W. Morgan,
                                              Acting Commissioner.
