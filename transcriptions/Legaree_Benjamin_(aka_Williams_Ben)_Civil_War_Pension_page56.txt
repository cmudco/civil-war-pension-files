              The above cited claim for pension under the act of

       May 11, 1912, filed May 8, 1917, is dismissed on the ground

       that it is a duplicate of the one filed July 24, 1916,

       jamin Lagree, in Co. B, 103 U. S. C. Infantry, and no

       new element has been brought into the case.
