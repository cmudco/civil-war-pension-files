was a mid-wife and attended nearly all
the women on the plantation in confinement.
She was with clt. when all her children
were born. My mother died January 1, 1880.
and I can only fix the dates of the births
of clts children by a record kept by my
mother. I know Jacob Jones, the youngest
child was about two years old when my
mother died, and the record I have in
my possession shows that Elizabeth Jones,
was born Dec. 17, 1873, that William Jones
was born February 23, 1876, and that the
boy Jacob Jones was born July 4, 1878.
A part of this record is in my own hand-
writing. I kept it for my mother.
I made an affidavit for Mrs. Susan
Jones in her claim for pension. That
affidavit was written by Mrs. E. A.
Doughty at Beaufort. I think it was
about two years ago. I did not
have the correct dates of the births
of these children with me at the time
I made that affidavit. I testified
from memory then. I gave the
dates as best I could without any-
thing to refer to. My only knowledge
of the exact dates of the births of these
children is from this record. I am
positive as to the date of the death
of my mother. She died January 1, 1880.
                                       Robert Goodwin
                                                      Deponent.

Sworn to and subscribed before me this 28th day of December,
1896, and I certify that the contents were fully made known to deponent before signing.
                                       W. H. Schucking
