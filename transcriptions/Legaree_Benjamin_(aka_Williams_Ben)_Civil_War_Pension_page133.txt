              Adams Run, S.C.
                            June 2, 1924.
Wm. Fletcher & Co., Esqs.,
                  Washington, D.C.
Gentlemen:-
          Mr. Ben Williams, being
very feeble requests me to say to the Govern-
ment through you, that he welcomes the
Committee if when it comes he is living.
As he is unable to know, and to arrange
as may be needed to get the Committee
from the Adams Run S.C. depot to him at
where he may be found near Slann's S.C.
just come up to the village to Rev. Wm. H.
Paden's and he will get you to me or me
to you without further or any expenses. He,
Rev. Paden know where to and will find me for
you. Respectfully submitted. Wm. H Paden by req
uest of Ben. Williams.

P. S. I am absolute-
ly certain Mr. Ben.
Williams is the man
he says he is
Signed W.H. Paden
