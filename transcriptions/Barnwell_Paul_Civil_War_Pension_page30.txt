6 I enlisted at Beaufort, S. C., in
1863, in the fall. I cant give the
exact date.
7 I served as a corporal in Co.
F- 33 U. S. C. T., which was my
only U. S. service.
8 I am sure I was not examined
at enlistment. They simply took my
height and that was all.
9 I served three years and some
months. I cant tell how many
months. I was discharged in the
spring of 1866. I was the only one
who enlisted when I did. Higginson
was my Col. Strong was Lt Col.
Strawbridge was Maj. Rogers was
my Capt. Stone was my 1" Lt. Judson
was 2" Lt. William Tompkins was
my Ord. Sergt. The Ord. Sergt. and
Sergt. Wiley McLennon were my tent
mates. Both are dead, Caesar Austin,
Frogmore, this Co., Fred Fripp, Beaufort
S. C. and James Brown Beaufort,
S. C. were all in my same Co.
10 We made up at Beaufort, S. C.
We remained there about a year and
then went to Fla. In Fla. we
visited Jacksonville, Camp Finnegan
and all round and then returned
to Beaufort. We next went to Morris
Island, S. C. We were around Charleston
until that city fell. We then went to
                                    Paul Barnwell
                                                Deponent.
