Case of        Ben Williams, alias Benjamin Legare      No. 1424473


        On this   20 day of August            , 19 24, at Savannah
county of    Chatham                  , State of    Georgia            before me,
    M.W.Vogtman            , a Special Examiner of the Bureau of Pensions,
personally appeared Timothy Judge               , who, being by me first
duly sworn to answer truly all interrogatories propounded to him
during this special examination of aforesaid claim for pension,
deposes and says:                                                                5-5121

 1      I am about 85 years of age, occupation, peddler, P.O. address
 2  R.F.D.1, I live on Jenks Ave. about three doors from Augusta Road,
 3  Savannah, Georgia.
 4      I served in Co. B, 103 U.S.C. Inf., I enlisted at Hiltonhead,
 5  S.C. and was discharged at Fort Palaski, Ga., I don't remember the
 6  date of my enlistment and discharge.  My Captain was named
 7  Henderson.
 8      It has been so long ago that I am unable to remember the names
 9  of all the men who served in my company and regiment and I do not
10  recall a man by the name of Ben or Benjamin Legree or Williams.
11      Q. I have related to you the story of this man's service in
12  the same company and regiment in which you served and I have
13  given you his personal description, are you now able to recall
14  him? A. No sir, I am unable to recall him, but those officers'
15  names that you read off are all familiar to me. Henderson,
16  Bogert and Manning were my leading officers. I did not take
17  part in the battle of Honey Hill. I stayed with my company
18  all the time and my company did not take part in that battle, the
19  33rd. I understand, was in that battle, I know our company was not
20  in it.
21      It is possible that if I were to see this claimant that I
22  would recall him, but I am unable to place him now.
23      I am not related and have no financial interest in the
24  prosecution of this claim.
25      I have heardthe foregoing deposition read, it is correct.
