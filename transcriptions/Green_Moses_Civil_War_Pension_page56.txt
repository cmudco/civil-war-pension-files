GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of South Carolina, County of Beaufort, ss:
In the matter of The pension claim of Richard Heyward
as guardian of minor of Moses Green.
ON THIS 2nd day of August A.D. 1891; personally appeared before me
a Notary Public in and for the aforesaid County duly authorized to administer oaths,
Patty Heyward aged 45 years, a resident of Beaufort Island
in the County of Beaufort and State of South Carolina
well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who, being duly sworn, declared in relation to aforesaid case
as follows:
That the soldier Moses Green died on
the 7th day February 1879 and on the follow-
ing May on the 18th day of the said month
I gave birth to the minor Moley Green and
that the said soldier Moses Green is
the father of the aforesaid minor Mil-
ey Green and that I am her mother.
That the minor Moley is the only sur-
viving child of the soldier. That the date
of birth as given 18th day of May 1879, is the
correct date and the one given as February
10th 1876 is a mistake on the part of my
local agent. That I am unable to furnish
record proof of date of death of soldier and
also record proof of birth of minor, for reason
that no such records are kept by the public authorities
Her Post Office address is Beaufort S.C.
She further declare s that she has no interest in said case and is not concerned
in its prosecution.

Augustus G. Bascomb                                 her
E.M. Pinckney                                 Patty X Heyward
