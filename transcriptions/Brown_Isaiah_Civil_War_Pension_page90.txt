                                    BEAUFORT, S. C.,

                                    Feb. 26  1934

Hon. E. W. Morgan
Director, Widows' and Dependants'
Claim Division                      In Re: Brown, Isaiah
                                         S. C. 598 425...MNN-Cb

Dear Sir:
Your letter under date of December 26th., 1933 has been duly received

and contants carefully noted.

In reply to same, you are advised that the explanation regarding the

decrepancy in the two bills on file are these: The amount of $110.00

was the original bill submitted by the daughter of the deceased veter

an, Isaiah Brown. The amount which I myself loan to the said daughter

amounts to $97.00. The difference in the amount of the funeral expenses

of the said Isaiah Brown was paid by the daughter of the veteran, Amy.

Wright , who was and is the Adminstratrix of the Estate of Isiah Brown.

For you further information, there were many bills submitted to the

said admistratrix, which did not cover the amount forwarded her some

time ago as a reinbursement..

I hope that this is sufficient information that you will reimburse me

for the amount advanced.  If there is further information regarding

same, That you desire I will gladly give same.

                               Yours very truly,

                               Cain Brown
