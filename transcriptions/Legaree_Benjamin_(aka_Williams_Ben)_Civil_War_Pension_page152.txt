                        Memorandum, S.E. Division.

      Subject.    No. 1424473.      Williams alias Legaree.

      Statement.  The claimant's statement is not sufficiently
comprehensive, and in detail, to permit of an opinion as to his
identity with the soldier.  As he alleges that he resided in the
vicinity of Savannah, Ga., before his enlistment, and his brother
and other comrades of the alleged service appear to have resided
there, further examination at that point may aid in developing
the essential information.

      Reference.  To Savannah, Chatham Co., Ga., for the testimony
of available witnesses to determine identity of claimant and the
soldier, as above indicated.
                                          R W T
                                          Reviewer, S.E.D.

June 26, 1924.
