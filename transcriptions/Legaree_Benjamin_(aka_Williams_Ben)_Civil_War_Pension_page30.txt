  I was born at Potter, S.C. about 1840. Enlisted as
Sergeant in the Civil War at Hilton Head S.C.
about 1863, serving as such over 2 ½ yrs. under Major
Gen. Bougee or Beauregard (Spelling by sound) and
my Lieutenant was Henderson over my Co.
I never received any pay except my little board.
I received an honorable discharge from my Co.
and had the Papers but through ignorance not
knowing what service they could be, misplaced
them; I may find them later.                                   -> John E
   my own dear brother ^Elijah Legree served in my same Co.,      270,1364 894

  He lived near Savannah Ga. and was about
there, ^someone told ^him what to do. Pompey Jenkins, who
served in the Spanish American War, seeing
me needy, told me a little what and how
to do. He knew I served in the Civil War.
  Titus Jenkins also knows it, and they have each
sworn to the same and sent it into the Bureau
for use. I really don't know of any man
in the United States of America, who would ever
try to get a pension for services he never ren-
dered. I would not try to do so. - over 3 -
