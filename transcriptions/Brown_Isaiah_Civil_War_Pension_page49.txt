Dear Madam:

          To complete your claim for reimbursement in the case of
Isaiah Brown, you should furnish the joint affidavit of two
credible persons who have knowledge of the facts to which they
testify, showing whether the claimant left a widow,or a child
under the age of 16 years at the date of his death, or whether
he left any money and the amount, or property - either real or
personal - and its character and value.
