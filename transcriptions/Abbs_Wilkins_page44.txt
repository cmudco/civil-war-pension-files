Sirs:

        In response to your inquiry dated the 20th instant, received the
same date, you are advised that above-cited claim for pension under
the act of June 27, 1890, requires claimant's sworn statement as to
when disease of rectum originated, and if same was contracted prior to
August 29, 1904, date of filing claim, he should furnish the best
evidence obtainable, medical if possible, showing existence and de-
gree of disability therefrom on date named.

                                                           Commissioner.
