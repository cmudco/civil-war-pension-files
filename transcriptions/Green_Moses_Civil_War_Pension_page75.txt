                                GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of So Carolina                      County of Beaufort                 , ss:

In the Pension Claim No.                   , of Patty Heyward wid of
Moses Green
late of Co., D of the 33 Reg't of U.S.C.T. Vols., personally came

before me, a Notary Public in and for the aforesaid County and State,
Richard Haywood , whom I hereby certify is a respectable and

credible person and who, being first duly sworn, deposes and says that his age is 49
That he was a member of Co D
33 Regt U.S.C.T. That in the month
of July 1863 while his company was
at Hilton Head. Moses Green began
to complain of heart trouble. and a
fluttering of heart. That he has known
the soldier before the war. and has seen
him with many attack of heart trouble -
has known when he could not take one
step. and often seen his heart beat as
if to jump out of his body. He was
not able to do much duty and was about
to be discharged on account of his sickness
before we were mustered out. He was very
intimately acquainted with soldier while in ~~co~~

He further declares that his post office address is Port Royal , County of
Beaufort , State of S C , and that he is not related to claimant,

interested in said claim, or concerned in its prosecution.
