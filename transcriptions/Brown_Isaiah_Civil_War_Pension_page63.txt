                       In Re: Isaiah Brown
                              S.C. 598426    ----MBAB

State of South Carolina

County of Beaufort

          Personally appeared before me, James Moultrie,

age 60 years, and   Rev. R. T. Frazier          age    74 years

who first being duly sworn depose and say: That they were

well acquainted with Isaiah Brown, deceased Civil War Veterans

S. C. 598424, That they have been so acquainted with the said

veterans for many many years; That they know as a matter of

fact that the said 'siah Brown is dead; That the know as a

matter of fact that the wife of the said Isiah Brown pre-

decased the said veteran  about a year and a half; That the

said Isaiah Brown did not leave surviving him any child or

children under the age of Sixteen years; That as far as the

affiants know or believe, the said Isaiah Brown did not leave

any amount of money nor did he leave any real or personal

property; That this affidavit is made by us of our own free

will and accord without fear, dread or intimidation; That we

are not interested in any way in the outcome of this affidavit.

Sworn to before me this             }   James Moultrie
                                    }
9th day of September, 1932          }   Rev R. T. Frazier
     Charles E. Washington
          Notary Public for S. C.

          Governor.
