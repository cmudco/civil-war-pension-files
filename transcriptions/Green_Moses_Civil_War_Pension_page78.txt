Affidavit.

State of South Carolina , County of Beaufort , ss:

In the Claim of Patty Haywood, widow of
Moses Green Co D, 33d U.S.C.T.
ON THIS 7th day of September , A. D. 1891, personally appeared
before me, a Notary Public , in and for the aforesaid County,
duly authorized to administer oaths, Charles Stapleton
aged 48 years, a resident of Port Royal Island , in the County of
Beaufort , and State of So Ca , whose Post Office address is
Beaufort S.C. and Josephine Davis
aged 49 years, a resident of Port Royal Island , in the County of
Beaufort , and State of So Ca , whose Post Office address is
Beaufort So Ca , well known to me to be respectable
and entitled to credit, and who being duly sworn, declare in relation to aforesaid case as follows:

Affiants should here state all the known facts material to the case, and how a knowledge thereof has been obtained.

that they and each of them knew the above
named Moses Green. He is now dead.
he died in the month of February 1879
at Old Fort near Beaufort S.C.
He died suddenly. We and each of us
have frequently heard him complain of
pains in his head. We believe that he
died from the same. He had been a
sufferer from head trouble for some time
before his death. His last illness was
of short duration.
There is no public record kept within
this County of death.
Death
      cont

We further declare that we have no interest in said claim, and are not concerned in its prosecution.

A. N. Ezekiel                         Charles Stapleton
