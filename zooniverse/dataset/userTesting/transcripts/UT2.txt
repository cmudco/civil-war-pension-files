GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of South Carolina, County of Colleton, ss:

In the matter of Hans Rahnaw (widow of Harry Rahnaw) Late Private Co. F 34 Regt U.S.C.T.

On this 18th day of March A.D. one thousand eight hundred and ninety-two, personally appeared before me C. B. Henderson, Master in Equity, within and for the aforesaid County and State, duly authorized to administer oaths, Cuffee Balles, aged 60 years, a resident of ____ County of Colleton and State of South Carolina, well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who, being duly sworn, declared in relation to the aforesaid case as follows:

that he was a private in Co. F 34 Regt U.S.C.T. with Harry Rahnaw, who he says was discharged for sickness about the month of June 1865, and that by Dr. Hansong Regimental Surgeon; this affiant being a close attendant during which sickness and did furnish Rahnaw in January 1876; affiant then present at the time of his death. He was then buried at the expense of affiant in Colleton County S.C. affiant further says claimant never drew a pension himself.

His Post-Office address is Green Pond, Colleton County S.C.
He further declares that he has no interest in said case and is not concerned in its prosecution.

[signature] Ernest Walker
[signature] Cuffee Balles (mark)
