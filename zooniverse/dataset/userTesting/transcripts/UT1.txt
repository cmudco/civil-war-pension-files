GENERAL AFFIDAVIT.

State of ________, County of ________, ss:

In the matter of Wilkins Abbe, Co. I 40th U.S.C.T.
Vols.

On this 16 day of November A.D. 1906 personally appeared before me, a Justice of the Peace in and for the aforesaid County and State, duly authorized to administer oaths, Malinda Abbe, aged 63 years, whose Post Office address is Imola Bolivar Co Miss, and Anna Lewis, aged 40 years, whose Post Office address is Bolivar, Bolivar Co Miss, well known to me to be reputable and entitled to credit, and who, being duly sworn, declared in relation to the aforesaid case as follows:

Some years ago, I think in 1899 or 1890—I was intimately known then from 1904—2 became incapacitated for hard work (I am a farmer) I was examined by the board of Bolivar Co who pronounced my disease some species of Rheumatism. The trouble was in the foot. I have grown worse and am now unable to do hard manual labor. I am now living with my son and I have ceased at time to farm myself as I am unable to do the work.

We further declare that we have known Abner and Charly West, have known Wilkins Abbe for more than thirty years and our personal knowledge know this statement about to be true.

We further declare that we have no interest in said case and are not concerned in its prosecution.

C. P. Lewis (40)
[signature] Wilkins Abbe

Affiant’s Signature.

[Stamp: Nov 28 1906, Pension Office]
