DECLARATION FOR INVALID PENSIONS.
[Act of June 27, 1890.]

State of [===Mississippi===], County of [===Bolivar===], ss:

On this 22 day of July A.D. one thousand eight hundred and ninety 1890, personally appeared before me [===W. H. Maxine===], a Notary Public, within and for the County and State aforesaid, [===Wilkins Abbe===], aged 49 years, a resident of the [===Delta Plantation===], County of [===Bolivar===], State of [===Mississippi===], who, being duly sworn according to law, declares that he is the identical [===Wilkins Abbe===], who was enrolled on the 29 day of July A.D. 1863, in Co. D of the Ninth Regiment of U.S. Col. Arty (heavy) Vols., in the war of the rebellion, and served at least ninety days, and was honorably discharged at [===Vicksburg, Mississippi===] on the 20 day of May 1866.

That he is now unable to earn a support by manual labor by reason of disease of the head & jaw bone from puling & while during the war.

That said disabilities are not due to his vicious habits, and are to the best of his knowledge and belief permanent.

That he has not applied for pension under application No. ______. That he is not a pensioner under certificate No. ______.

That he makes this declaration for the purpose of being placed on the pension-roll of the United States under the provisions of the act of June 27, 1890. He hereby appoints, with full power of substitution and revocation, [===TUCKER, EVANS & MANOGUE===], [===Washington, D.C.===], his true and lawful attorneys to prosecute his claim; and he hereby promises and agrees to pay his said attorneys the sum of ten ($10) dollars for their services herein under the provisions of said Act.

That his Post Office address is [===Maxime===], County of [===Bolivar===], State of [===Mississippi===].

[===Wilkins Abbe===] (his mark)
(Claimant’s Signature)

Attest:
[===George Hood===]
[===M. Green===]
